Sandsnipper Shore - a Mollusk OS launcher theme

Layout: the stock FunKey theme's layout.xml from FunKey-OS, with an animated background
(<animatedImage>), new colors, bars and icons, as in the koi themes.

Sand, sea, sandpipers, crabs and finds: drawn and animated by HourlySword
(Copyright (c) 2026 HourlySword). Each of the 36 frames is rendered from an HTML canvas
scene, posed so that the loop repeats exactly.

System icons: the FunKey-OS theme device pictures, recolored, with a soft halo;
the arcade, PICO-8 and Pokemon Mini icons are the koi themes' own.
Font: Open Sans Bold by Steve Matteson (Apache License 2.0).
Sounds: the Classic theme's sounds (theme by TimmyB).
