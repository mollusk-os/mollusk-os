Leafcutter Trail - a Mollusk OS launcher theme

Layout: the stock FunKey theme's layout.xml from FunKey-OS (theme by TimmyB), with an
animated background (<animatedImage>), new colors, bars and icons, as in the koi themes.

Forest floor, ants, leaf pieces and mushrooms: drawn and animated by HourlySword
(Copyright (c) 2026 HourlySword). Each of the 36 frames is rendered from an HTML canvas
scene, posed so that the loop repeats exactly.

System icons: the FunKey-OS theme device pictures (theme by TimmyB), recolored, with a
soft halo; the arcade, PICO-8 and Pokemon Mini icons are the koi themes' own.
Font: Cabin Bold by Pablo Impallari (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
Sounds: the Classic theme's sounds (theme by TimmyB).
  Highlight.wav by orginaljun - orginaljun.deviantart.com (CC BY 4.0)
  http://www.freesound.org/people/orginaljun/sounds/157871/
