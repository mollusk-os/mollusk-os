Pika Peak, a launcher theme for Mollusk OS

The art made for Mollusk OS is by HourlySword and is licensed under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

It is:
- the animated background, animations/pikas.rgb565;
- the images in images/ and the splash screen, splash.xml;
- the arcade, PICO-8 and Pokemon Mini icons, and the recoloring and halo of
  the other system icons;
- the changes to the stock FunKey layout in layout.xml.

The other parts keep their own terms:
- layout.xml starts from the stock FunKey theme of FunKey-OS, and the other
  system icons are its device pictures (theme by TimmyB);
- the sounds are the Classic theme's (theme by TimmyB); Highlight.wav is by
  orginaljun, under CC BY 4.0;
- the font, Cabin Bold, is under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: see OFL.txt.

Credits.txt says who made what.
