Koi Glass - a Mollusk OS koi theme (generated by tools/koi-themes)

Layout: the stock FunKey theme's layout.xml from FunKey-OS (theme by TimmyB), with an
animated background (<animatedImage>), new colors, bars and icons.

Koi, water, lily pads, water lilies, ripples: by HourlySword.
The koi are drawn by the koi renderer from my other projects, and the pond's
colors and shapes follow the pond designs there.
Water caustics: the function of Mollusk OS's tools/make-caustics-animation.py.

System icons: the FunKey-OS theme device pictures (theme by TimmyB), recolored, with a
soft halo; the arcade, PICO-8 and Pokemon Mini icons are new.
Font: Open Sans Bold by Steve Matteson (Apache License 2.0).
Sounds: the Classic theme's sounds (theme by TimmyB).
  Highlight.wav by orginaljun - orginaljun.deviantart.com (CC BY 4.0)
  http://www.freesound.org/people/orginaljun/sounds/157871/
